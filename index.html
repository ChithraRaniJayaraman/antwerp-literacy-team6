Change begins with the people who choose to move first.

This project is not about us.

It is about schools willing to explore, teachers willing to experiment, and students discovering new ways to learn.

Our role is simple - to stand beside them, to ask the right questions, and to help turn small shifts into lasting change.

We did not come to lead the story. We came to understand it.

In this project, the real protagonists are the schools - the teachers who stayed curious, the students who kept trying, and the leaders who chose to rethink what learning can be.

We listened. We connected. We supported the momentum that was already there.

Because meaningful change does not start from outside - it grows from within.

Why this challenge cannot be ignored

Change is already happening — quietly.

Across schools, reading is becoming less frequent, less engaging, and less meaningful for many students.

This is not just about performance. It affects how students think, learn, and grow.

We started by understanding this reality — not with assumptions, but by looking closely at what is actually happening in schools.

And from there, a clear direction began to form:

A deeper understanding of reading challenges in Flemish secondary schools

Goals grounded in real school improvement research

Practical approaches: Self-Regulated Learning, Peer Tutoring, and Parental Involvement

A phased plan that grows steadily over five years

Ready-to-use tools, templates, and guides for immediate use

We are not here to prescribe solutions.

We are here to support schools with the knowledge, tools, and confidence to shape change in their own way.
