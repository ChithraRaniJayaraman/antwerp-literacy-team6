The Reading Problem

A clear-eyed look at reading decline, motivation, and why Antwerp’s schools need a different approach.

What the document says

  • Teenagers in Antwerp are reading less and with less joy.
  • Flanders dropped more than 20 points in reading performance compared to earlier PISA cycles.
  • More than 1 in 4 Belgian 15-year-olds do not have basic reading skills.
  • Nearly 1 in 3 students in Flanders says they only read when they absolutely have to.

Why it matters

  • Reading unlocks mathematics, science, history, and every other subject.
  • It builds vocabulary, empathy, and critical thinking.
  • In multilingual Antwerp, Dutch reading is central to school participation and social inclusion.
  • The challenge is structural, not just individual.
