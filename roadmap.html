Roadmap

A five-year implementation plan: pilot, scale, then sustain.

Phase 1

Year 1 • Pilot and Build Foundation
  • Select pilot schools.
  • Assign a reading coordinator.
  • Train the first group of teachers.
  • Run the reading-interest survey.
  • Begin peer tutoring and one family evening.

Phase 2

Years 2-3 • Scale and Embed
  • Expand to all schools.
  • Use pilot teachers as trainers.
  • Set daily reading time.
  • Host regular family evenings.
  • Track progress with student and teacher tools.

Phase 3

Years 4-5 • Sustain and Institutionalize
  • Embed reading in improvement plans.
  • Integrate strategies into onboarding.
  • Use experienced teachers as internal trainers.
  • Keep tracking equity and engagement.
  • Celebrate milestones.
