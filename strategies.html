Strategies

Three interdependent goals and three practical approaches from the blueprint.

Goal 1

Build teacher capacity through practical strategies, time, and collegial support.

Goal 2

Build a school-wide reading culture so reading becomes visible across the whole day.

Goal 3

Build student ownership so reading becomes something students set goals for and reflect on.

Approach 1: Students take the wheel

Students choose what to read, set goals, track their progress, and reflect on what they discover.

Approach 2: Reading together

Older students read with younger ones, classmates work in pairs, and peers discuss meaning together.

Approach 3: Families as reading partners

Family involvement increases motivation and makes reading feel like part of life, not only school.
